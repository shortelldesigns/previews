Captain's log

A neighborhood kitchen.

"I honestly feel like I'm cooking for the community," says owner Danielle Holliday. The pirate theme is a nod to the hometown Moapa Valley Pirates.

  • 2015Pirates Landing opens on Moapa Valley Blvd.
  • 2021Danielle Holliday takes the helm, keeping the crew and the house recipes.
  • TodayThe only restaurant in Logandale, and business has doubled, if not tripled, since before COVID.
