About

Your neighborhood pool crew.

Moapa Valley Pool Services is a locally owned pool cleaning, maintenance and repair service in Moapa Valley.

Budget-billing customers pay one flat rate based on 40 yearly visits. Most get 42 to 50 visits a year, and if you get fewer than 40 in 12 billing cycles, your next filter cleaning is free. Invoices can be paid online, by card, check or money order, and referrals earn a discount.
