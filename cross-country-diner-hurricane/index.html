Our story

Two chefs, two trailers, one great diner.

Sergio Arroyo (from Guadalajara) and Sylvia Arroyo (from Michigan) are both chefs. They turned two car-hauling trailers into a 50s diner and filled the menu with food from all over the map.

  • 2019Cross Country Diner opens on State Street in Hurricane, in July.
  • 2021Climate-controlled 1950s-style dining room added, with booths, music and decor.
  • TodayIndoor and outdoor seating, daily specials, and full Halloween and Christmas makeovers.
