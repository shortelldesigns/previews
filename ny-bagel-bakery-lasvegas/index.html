The bagels

Made the New York way.

These are New York-style bagels: boiled, then baked, for that crackly crust and chewy middle. The ovens get going at 3 AM every day, so the bagels are fresh when the doors open.

Inside, it's a classic NY bagel shop with art and memorabilia from home on the walls, and a family from the Bronx behind the counter.
