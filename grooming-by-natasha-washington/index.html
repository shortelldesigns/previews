Meet Natasha

Grooming runs in the family.

Natasha is a third-generation groomer, following her mother and grandmother. She has 15 years of hands-on grooming experience and keeps up with new styles and safety practices. Her salon is quiet and mellow on purpose, so nervous pups can relax.
