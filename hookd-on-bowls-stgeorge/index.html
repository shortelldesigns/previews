About

Built your way.

Hook'd on Bowls serves build-your-own bowls from a food truck in St. George: pick rice or noodles, chicken or beef, then your sauce.

The team caters too. One couple hired the truck for their wedding reception and said it handled dietary needs and was well-prepared on the day.
