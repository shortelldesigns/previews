Meet the pitmaster

Chef Frank Ford.

Frank is the owner and executive chef behind Smokin' Blues. He's a Le Cordon Bleu graduate who has cooked for troops in Iraq, and now he brings that training to smoked meat in southern Utah. Customers say he's light-handed with smoke and seasoning, so you taste the meat.
