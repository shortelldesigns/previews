About

A neighborhood salon on Tropicana.

Lupita Beauty Salon is a full-service salon at 4425 East Tropicana Avenue, Suite B.

It offers hair, barber, spa, nail and makeup services under one roof, with products from brands like Matrix Biolage, ISO, CHI and Paul Mitchell.
