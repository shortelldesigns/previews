About

Washington's local laundromat.

Washington Laundromat has been serving the community for over 35 years, and every washer and dryer was replaced with brand-new machines in 2023.

It's open 24 hours a day, every day, so you can do laundry before work, after the kids are asleep, or whenever the week allows.
