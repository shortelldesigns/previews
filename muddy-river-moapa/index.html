Our story

A Moapa roadhouse for decades.

The Muddy River has fed this corner of the valley for decades. Owner Kent Slight took over in 2007 and has kept the doors open ever since: a small, family-run diner with breakfast every day and dinner on the weekend.

Grab a table with a view, say hi to the regulars, and stay for a cold one at the bar. Coming from Logandale, Overton or the freeway, it's worth the drive.
