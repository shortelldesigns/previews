Meet Patty

Clinical know-how, a gentle touch.

Skinsational is owned and run by Patty Johnson, a licensed esthetician and retired surgical nurse who brought decades of experience, including years in Beverly Hills, to Mesquite.

Every visit starts with your skin: what it needs, what to expect, and what you can skip. Clients say she explains everything, respects their privacy, and never pushes extra products.
